TAJ MAHAL DESCRIPTION

Taj Mahal is an ivory-white marble mausoleum located on the right bank of the Yamuna River in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India.

  • Built by: Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan
  • Built for: His beloved wife, Mumtaz Mahal, who died in childbirth in 1631
  • Construction/ Period: Begun in 1631 and completed around 1653 (the main tomb was finished by 1648)
  • Lead Architect: Ustad Ahmad Lahori
  • Design & Style: A masterwork of Mughal architecture, blending Islamic, Persian, Ottoman, and Indian design traditions
